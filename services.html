Core Service Divisions
08 Divisions
01
Strategic Advisory

Planning & Development Strategy

Every transaction involving land or physical assets is governed by planning frameworks. Engaging expert guidance is the defining factor in unlocking the highest and best-use permissions — directly dictating a site's valuation, viability, and capital execution.

Our planning strategists guide institutional and private clients through contemporary zoning and municipal processes. Our scope encompasses formal applications and complex appeals, compulsory purchase orders, compensation recovery, strategic urban regeneration, heritage evaluations, infrastructure payment modelling, stakeholder diplomacy, and environmental sustainability frameworks.

By blending cross-border reach with deep local market intelligence, we dissolve complex regulatory challenges to finalise approvals for high-density, multi-use developments.

02
Development

Development Lifecycle & Transactions

From institutional valuations, strategic land acquisitions, and site disinvestments to structural development funding and global portfolio advisory, our development ecosystem services residential, commercial, rural, and mixed-use projects worldwide.

Supported by an interconnected grid of development specialists, we customise our operational frameworks to align with the specific risks and mandates of landowners, corporate developers, occupiers, and institutional funds.

We serve as active partners through every phase of the asset lifecycle. Backed by proprietary, forward-looking market analytics, we insulate our clients against shifting cyclical trends and macro challenges across the international real estate landscape.

03
Capital Markets

Capital Markets & Investment Advisory

With specialised transaction teams operating across every sector of the global built environment, we deploy institutional capital and advise sovereign funds, family offices, and private clients on premier investment opportunities worldwide.

Whether your capital mandate targets prime workspace, dominant retail centres, logistics hubs, healthcare facilities, agricultural acreage, or premium residential blocks, we deploy dedicated sector specialists to optimise your capital deployment at every lifecycle stage.

Our transaction fluency is supported by a market-leading research division that cross-references predictive trend data to de-risk decisions across commercial and residential classes.

04
Consultancy

Strategic Advisory & Specialist Consultancy

As recognised subject-matter experts, our global consultancy teams resolve highly complex operational and structural requirements across all institutional and private asset classes.

Harnessing specialised industry forensics, decades of transaction history, and macroeconomic data, our consultants author bespoke strategies that transform real estate portfolios into resilient income engines.

Our cross-functional capabilities span architectural innovation, heritage preservation, corporate tax planning, and development project management — governing the full lifecycle from master-planning and feasibility modelling through to physical delivery and handover.

05
Private Client

Private Client Acquisitions

Navigating the high-stakes UK and international property market requires exceptional precision — whether you are acquiring a prime capital residence, a historic countryside estate, or high-yielding off-market assets.

Our private client advisors offer completely bespoke acquisition strategies and unmatched market intelligence. We guide you seamlessly through every step of the underwriting, negotiation, and settlement process, leveraging off-market networks to secure your ideal asset.

06
Disposals

Portfolio Disposals & Asset Divestment

Achieving maximum value in asset divestments requires deep local execution paired with global distribution. We leverage data-led research and predictive analytics to ensure a frictionless, high-value transaction process.

Whether you are liquidating a legacy private residence or exiting a major residential investment block, our team provides transparent institutional underwriting, elite global marketing exposure, and highly managed viewing environments to defend your margins.

07
Finance

Real Estate Finance & Capital Structuring

Whether you are financing a primary luxury residence, structuring a buy-to-let portfolio, or syndicating debt for cross-border commercial assets, our structured finance division engineers intelligent, optimised capital stacks based on deep macroeconomic insights.

Our relationships operate at local, national, and international tiers, managed by debt and equity specialists with extensive tenures in corporate banking and private credit markets.

We act as an exclusive advocate for our clients, maintaining deep institutional alliances with tier-one clearing banks, institutional funds, and alternative private lenders to execute swift, cost-effective financing facilities.

08
Research

Proprietary Research & Macro Analytics

We deliver independent, institutional-grade research, thematic analysis, and econometric forecasting to global stakeholders across commercial, residential, and rural property sub-sectors.

Each specialised data team possesses an unrivalled reputation for publishing precision market intelligence, timely cyclical analysis, and authoritative commentary.

As a completely autonomous and independent research institution, our counsel remains strictly objective and definitive. We customise our research mandates uniquely for each project, serving landowners, developers, asset managers, housing associations, investment banks, and public sector authorities.
