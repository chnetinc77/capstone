X1 Trafford Waters | Off-Plan in Trafford City

Overview

Trafford Waters, a brand-new neighbourhood located in the heart of Trafford City is set to redefine urban living through one of the North's most exciting transformation masterplans. Set across 100 acres of regenerated waterfront, Trafford Waters will deliver over 3,000 new homes, retail, leisure and commercial space. X1 Trafford Waters represents early-stage access to one of Greater Manchester's most ambitious urban regeneration projects. Investors benefit from significant capital appreciation potential as the masterplan completes alongside strong rental demand driven by proximity to MediaCityUK and Old Trafford.
