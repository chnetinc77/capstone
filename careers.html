The Role

A fantastic opportunity has arisen for highly motivated individuals with a proven sales track record to join our dynamic team. Our ethos is all about being up for the challenge. If you have the drive to pursue a career — not just another sales role — we look forward to meeting you. The ideal candidate will be switched on, ambitious, and motivated by uncapped earning potential, with a great deal of integrity, energy, and drive, as well as the desire to exceed targets. As the face of the company, you should have the ability to build genuine rapport and truly understand our clients' aspirations.

Overview
  • Your primary responsibility will be to coordinate the exchange and completion of a number of properties within required timeframes, demanding exceptional organisational skills and the ability to prioritise under pressure.
  • You will liaise with buyers, vendors, solicitors and mortgage brokers — strong customer service and communication skills, both verbal and written, are therefore essential.
  • Strong physical and electronic administration skills are required for this varied and interesting role.
  • The ability to write creative advertisements, newsletters, blogs, and brochure copy will be a distinct advantage.
  • Excellent IT proficiency including Microsoft Word and Excel is essential.
  • Good customer relations skills and the ability to work collaboratively as part of a team are key requirements.
  • Experience in — or a willingness to learn — the company CRM system to its fullest capability.
Key Selection Criteria
  • Ability to quickly build rapport and strong relationships with clients, the team and developers
  • Creative problem-solving skills and the ability to think laterally
  • Excellent IT skills including advanced Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Adobe Acrobat
  • Professional yet personable manner
  • An excellent command of English with the ability to draft correspondence and contracts
  • Strong attention to detail with the ability to proofread accurately
  • Fast and accurate typing speed
  • Ability to work both independently and as part of a supervised team
  • Going the extra mile to deliver exceptional service in a timely manner
  • Candidate must align with our core ethos: 100% investor satisfaction, under-promising and over-delivering
Main Duties
  • Operating and maintaining the client database
