Off-Plan

We provide access to a carefully curated portfolio of off-plan developments across the UK's most dynamic growth markets. By securing allocations directly with developers, we offer our clients preferential pricing, flexible payment structures, and the potential for significant capital appreciation prior to completion.

Our due diligence process is rigorous — every development is assessed for location fundamentals, developer track record, build quality, and yield projections before being presented to our investors.

Off-Market

Some of the most compelling investment opportunities never reach the open market. Through our established relationships with developers, landowners, and institutional vendors, Capstone Invest Group is able to source and present exclusive off-market properties to our investor base.

These opportunities often offer superior pricing and reduced competition, providing investors with a distinct advantage in the acquisition process.

Corporate Relocation

Our Selection Criteria

Every Property Must Pass Our
Four-Point Assessment.

Our mandate is to protect investor capital while maximising return potential. Each opportunity is stress-tested across four independent dimensions before it is ever presented to our network.

Market Analysis

Supply/demand imbalances, demographic shifts, transport improvements.

Developer Review

Auditing developer balance sheets, past completion records, and structural quality.

Stress Testing

Yield modelling against severe rate shifts and extended void periods.

Exit Strategy

Verifying secondary market liquidity for optimal exit opportunities.
