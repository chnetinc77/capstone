Planning & Development Strategy

Every transaction involving land or physical assets is governed by planning frameworks. Our planning strategists guide institutional and private clients through contemporary zoning and municipal processes — encompassing formal applications, complex appeals, compulsory purchase orders, heritage evaluations, and environmental sustainability frameworks.

Development Lifecycle & Transactions

From institutional valuations, strategic land acquisitions, and site disinvestments to structural development funding and global portfolio advisory, our development ecosystem services residential, commercial, rural, and mixed-use projects worldwide. We serve as active partners through every phase of the asset lifecycle.

Capital Markets & Investment Advisory

With specialised transaction teams operating across every sector of the global built environment, we deploy institutional capital and advise sovereign funds, family offices, and private clients on premier investment opportunities worldwide. Our transaction fluency is supported by a market-leading research division.

Strategic Advisory & Specialist Consultancy

As recognised subject-matter experts, our global consultancy teams resolve highly complex operational and structural requirements across all institutional and private asset classes. Our cross-functional capabilities span architectural innovation, heritage preservation, corporate tax planning, and development project management.

Private Client Acquisitions

Navigating the high-stakes UK and international property market requires exceptional precision. Our private client advisors offer completely bespoke acquisition strategies and unmatched market intelligence, guiding you through every step of underwriting, negotiation, and settlement, leveraging off-market networks.

Portfolio Disposals & Asset Divestment

Achieving maximum value in asset divestments requires deep local execution paired with global distribution. We leverage data-led research and predictive analytics to ensure a frictionless, high-value transaction process — from legacy private residences to major residential investment blocks.

Real Estate Finance & Capital Structuring

Whether financing a luxury residence, structuring a buy-to-let portfolio, or syndicating debt for cross-border commercial assets, our structured finance division engineers intelligent, optimised capital stacks. We maintain deep institutional alliances with tier-one clearing banks and private lenders.

Proprietary Research & Macro Analytics

We deliver independent, institutional-grade research, thematic analysis, and econometric forecasting to global stakeholders across commercial, residential, and rural property sub-sectors. As a completely autonomous research institution, our counsel remains strictly objective and definitive.
